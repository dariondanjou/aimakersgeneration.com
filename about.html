About us

Built by makers.For makers.

AI MAKERS GENERATION started as a reason to get out of the house — two working AI creative professionals in Atlanta who wanted to connect with fellow AI creators. It grew into a community that shares resources, catches up on AI news, and collaborates on the future.

Who we are

Meet the co-founders

Darion D'Anjou

Co-Founder

Darion D'Anjou is an award-winning writer, director, AI creative architect, trainer, and speaker based in Atlanta, and a co-founder of AI MAKERS GENERATION. Through his studio, Darion D'Anjou, he delivers world-class creative and technical solutions for small and mid-sized businesses, Fortune 500 companies, and government organizations — including CNN, Coca-Cola, AT&T Time Warner, HBO, Disney, Marvel Studios, Sony Pictures Entertainment, Nike, and the CDC. His science-fiction short Pony screened and won awards at festivals in Atlanta, New York, Los Angeles, London, and Berlin, and his one-take horror short Mommy has drawn over 1.8 million views online. As a visual effects trainer at Digital Domain — the multi-award-winning studio founded by James Cameron — he contributed to Academy Award– and Emmy-nominated productions including Spider-Man: No Way Home, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, and HBO's The Last of Us. Darion now serves as a generative AI artist for Meta, where his generations were featured by Mark Zuckerberg during the 2025 debut of Meta Vibes.

Gheri Thomas

Co-Founder

Gheri Thomas is a classically trained fashion illustrator and designer, and a co-founder of AI MAKERS GENERATION. She holds a B.F.A. in Fashion Design from the Savannah College of Art and Design, where she tutored peers in rendering and digital concept creation. Across more than a decade in practice she has spanned illustration, branding, and technical design — creating fashion flats for established brands and custom work for notable clients. Today Gheri blends that traditional foundation with generative AI image tools, merging human craft with machine-generated creativity to make emotionally resonant, distinctive work. Through her studio, the Gheri Thomas Collective, her philosophy is simple: creativity isn't just practiced, it's pushed to its edges. She co-founded AI MAKERS GENERATION to help other artists cross that same bridge — to treat AI not as a threat to their craft, but as a new brush to build with confidently.

What we believe

Build the future. Share the knowledge.

AI is the biggest shift in creative work in a generation — and nobody should have to navigate it alone. AIMG exists so Atlanta's creatives, builders, and career-changers have a room to walk into: Film Bar AI every Tuesday evening, hands-on Workshop Wednesdays twice a month at Georgia Tech ATDC, and a four-week AI filmmaking intensive for people ready to go all-in.

Everything is beginner-friendly, in person, and honest — no hype, no gatekeeping. Come as you are, bring a laptop, and leave with something you made.
