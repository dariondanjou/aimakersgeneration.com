Welcome to the October 2026 Film Cohort. Today we roll. We are genuinely excited to have you in the room, and we can't wait to see what you make.

One honest heads-up: this cohort is fast-paced and intensive. Four Saturdays, one finished film. You'll choose your project in the first session and screen it for the room in the last. In between we'll move quickly through film grammar, agentic workflows, dialogue, editing, node-based pipelines, and commercials. It's a lot, and that's the point. Bring your curiosity and your ideas, and don't worry about getting it perfect on day one. Every one of us started where you are, and we'll be right there with you, shot by shot.
