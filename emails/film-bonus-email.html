Five lessons to use before Saturday
1
Lock the look before you prompt
A Midjourney personalization profile (or one dissected film still per scene) turns a one-line prompt into a film still. Without it you get a stock photo.
2
One beat per shot
Movies change angle every two or three seconds. Give each generation one camera, one action, and “no music or score.”
3
Make a sheet for anything that drifts
Location, logo, poster, sleeves, shoes. Add the sheet and rerun the generation instead of patching it with a video edit.
4
Give the eye a place to rest
One subject, negative space, focus that makes optical sense, light from real sources, and a narrow color palette. If the mailman matters, give him his own shot.
5
Write dialogue for the model
Name the mood, put a small action between lines, and rewrite anything the AI misreads. Re-voice each character in ElevenLabs so no one sounds like a stock AI voice.
