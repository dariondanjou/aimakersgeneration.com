This week’s homework
1
A minute of new capstone footage (video)
Push your capstone forward: make at least one minute of new footage we can critique. Put the session’s notes to work — vary your shots instead of staying wide, pull a real style reference so it never reads as “default AI,” scene-stitch for seamless motion, cut clean (no dissolves), and keep your backgrounds alive.
2
A shot-list breakdown (document)
Pick a one-minute scene from a film or commercial you love and break it down shot by shot. Screenshot every shot in order and, beside each, write what it is — shot size (wide, medium, close-up, insert), the camera move, and what it does for the story. Build it as a document using the Week 2 guide and template on your student page (they show you exactly how to lay it out).
3
Check before you generate
Before you spend credits on video, drop your stills / key frames into the cohort WhatsApp group for a quick thumbs-up. Fixing a frame is cheap; regenerating a bad clip is not.
4
Turn in both
Drag and drop both files — your footage and your shot-list document — into the This Week box on your student profile. If the video is too big to upload, paste a YouTube, Vimeo, or Google Drive link into the box instead.
